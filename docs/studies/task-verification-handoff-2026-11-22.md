# Product objective and boundaries

The original vision is to assess an immutable deliverable against explicitly supplied, versioned acceptance criteria, identify missing or unverifiable outcomes, and provide actionable feedback. ReviewRouter is the first consumer; Orchestrator is a later consumer. The portable capability returns facts and advisory assessments. Work Coordination retains terminal Work authority.

For the current experiment, Iliya is the report reader and decision owner. Hassan prepares and runs the bounded research. A third independent expert is not required. Independent expected behavior means that reviewer output does not define its own correct answer.

Retain these boundaries:

- Criteria come from the task owner. The model may explain a criterion but cannot silently add obligations, drop a clause or weaken the requested outcome.

- Ordinary OCR findings remain available. A criteria result neither overrides them nor automatically confirms them.

- Results apply to the exact candidate, criteria and assessment profile. Publication, permissions, credentials and final product decisions remain with their existing owners.

- The reviewer reads the candidate. Existing authorized runners produce execution evidence when needed.

- Specification generation, automatic implementation, unrestricted test generation, merge gating, Work completion, a new service/database/queue and a stable cross-product SDK are outside this research.

## Adapt the donor policy to our decision

Kodus deliberately limits demands for downstream proof, including whether a route consumes a changed serializer. Its skill also requires task-grounded requirements, contradiction checks and manual-check outcomes when an unseen helper decides a rule. These are reasonable choices for its low-noise gap-review policy. If our authored criterion explicitly requires the connection between components, we must inspect that connection. This is a policy difference, not a measured claim that Kodus would fail our C case.

Its optional critic can turn a refuted gap into “met” without adding positive evidence. Its production reader uses the PR branch reference rather than an immutable commit, and some read failures become absent material. These are concrete reasons to adapt the mechanism instead of inheriting its semantics.

The gap checker also scrutinizes negative and uncertain conclusions more than positive ones. A confidently incorrect positive conclusion may never trigger further reading.

# Choose one assessment strategy

| Strategy | Choose it when | Main risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reproducible input packet | Relevant allowed source and ordinary context fit a bounded packet using a simple selection rule. | Expert file selection can become hidden recurring work or omit a decisive dependency. |
| Bounded requirement-led source reading | Relevant context is scattered and obtaining it on demand is simpler than preparing an expert-selected packet. | Search can miss needed behavior and adds requests; the model may stop confidently too early. |

Select the cheapest plausible strategy from actual preparation needs before paid execution. This chooses the next hypothesis to test, not a proven winning architecture. Do not automatically implement both strategies or run a two-by-three comparison matrix.

An existing OCR/check report is the practical baseline and a legitimate final outcome for criteria it actually helps resolve. It is not a general substitute when ordinary checks leave an omitted requirement untested. Existing machine observations may be useful without a bespoke criterion-to-test mapper. The original options study explicitly allows the simpler semantic reviewer when meaningful CI coverage is sparse. [Original options and switching conditions](https://github.com/agent-teams-ai/engineering-research/blob/fa207e0111cea294378c57a0561ee4bb15591166/docs/studies/task-verification-five-practical-options-2026-11-11.md).

Combining ordinary review and criterion assessment inside one OCR invocation remains a later optimization. It first needs a supported criterion-result contract and evidence that ordinary review quality is preserved. A panel of critics and automatic test generation are not default next steps.

The preferred workflow is:

1. Capture the authored criteria and immutable candidate.

2. Assemble ordinary task context and a bounded source packet using a declared rule; use bounded reading where that is the selected simpler acquisition strategy.

3. Display ordinary OCR findings and existing check observations with their actual scope.

4. Reuse existing, explicitly justified check mappings where available. Do not manufacture a universal criterion-to-check mapper or interpret a green suite as coverage of every obligation.

5. Assess each unresolved requirement against the available source and applicable observations. Obtain necessary source before concluding when the selected reader allows it.

6. Return a supported conclusion or a specific missing-evidence request.

7. Let Iliya select feedback. Bind the correction and recheck to the unchanged requirements and new candidate.

An advisory positive judgment may be based on source inspection when that source establishes the relevant property. Environment-dependent or execution-dependent properties need the corresponding observations. Label the basis: observed behavior, source-based assessment or human decision. Before a positive conclusion, explain how the cited material establishes the stated outcome, including required wiring across components when relevant. Inspect material contrary evidence within that scope. A valid source reference proves provenance, not the semantic conclusion. This is an inspectable judgment, not a formal proof or a universal call-graph requirement.

# Keep source investigation and execution narrow

Bounded reading can be the selected initial strategy when it reduces ordinary preparation; it need not wait for a failed fixed-packet prototype. Within that strategy, source acquisition can happen before the final verdict. Do not trigger every read only after the model admits uncertainty: a confident but unsupported positive would bypass that rule. The model should investigate the specific basis needed for the authored requirement, not search indefinitely for every possible defect.

If bounded source reading is selected, use the already inspected OCR Behavior/Impact interfaces to decide whether a narrow adapter can expose actual observations or resolvable source while preserving candidate identity, acquisition errors and limits. The pinned CLI rejects analysis with managed mode and its report is Markdown; do not reopen a broad engine audit. Changing mode is not an unchanged-comparator experiment. Its built-in token limit can overshoot and does not replace pre-dispatch spend control.

A model-generated investigation summary is a claim. Feeding only that summary to another assessor does not reproduce direct source evidence or establish independent verification.

If engine reuse requires a substantial Go/output-contract redesign, a small fixed application sequence through existing ports may be cheaper: request a bounded named source, read it at the frozen candidate, make a bounded follow-up assessment, then stop. Do not add a new agent runtime or arbitrary tool scheduler. Select this only when ordinary preparation or a concrete source gap justifies it.

The source reader should distinguish read success, actual absence at the candidate, exclusion/not inspected, access failure and exhausted limits. A failed search is not proof that the behavior is absent.

Evaluate the one selected strategy on the new family. Compare another acquisition strategy only if repeated manual selection, inaccessible decisive context or material reader work leaves a consequential choice unresolved. Such a comparison has its own bounded question; it is not an automatic two-strategy matrix.

## When a targeted execution probe is justified

Use a small probe only when an identified behavioral question cannot be settled from the available source and existing observations. Freeze the expected external behavior from the authored requirement, use the same probe across the incomplete and corrected candidates, and count its preparation. A probe that asserts one preferred private helper or implementation shape can reject an equally valid implementation even if it fails on A and passes on B.

Keep setup failure, missing prerequisites and product failure distinct. Generated but unexecuted tests are not observations. Reuse an authorized runner; this does not require a generic test generator or a second verification platform.

# Measure useful decisions and recurring work

For each important requirement, retain the raw assessment, whether its evidence supports the conclusion, and what action the reader actually selected.

| Question | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Did it help? | New justified correction, necessary specific evidence request, justified completion, or a concrete reduction of required interpretation work. |
| Did it harm? | Unsupported satisfaction, unnecessary required rework or evidence requests, an inherited incorrect claim, or suppression of a valid OCR finding. |
| What did preparation require? | Automatic inputs, normal author context, manual file choices, criterion/check mappings and bespoke probe work; separate one-off setup. |
| What did the reader still do? | Additional source/log reading, questions and author knowledge needed to decide. |
| What did execution cost? | Every forwarded request, failed attempt, token, elapsed time and actual recorded charge. |

The baseline must not receive a perfect human-written answer disguised as free deterministic evidence. Conversely, a well-formatted model report is not useful merely because it is structured.

One Iliya-read trace can show concrete assistance. It cannot establish a general productivity improvement, and rereading a familiar case is not an unbiased speed measurement.

Keep criterion correctness, evidential support, mechanical validity and candidate applicability distinguishable. The M2 scorer's supplied semantic-support judgments are evaluator material, not an automatically available runtime oracle. A valid file reference does not by itself prove the cited claim.

# Stopping rules and the final recommendation

Freeze the question, acquisition rule, effective assessment profile, expected behavior and limits before the batch. Keep the model and effort fixed while testing the mechanism; a cheaper-model comparison is a later separate question.

Allow **one bounded correction round for the batch**, not one new round for every discovered issue. Preserve the original outputs. A changed prompt, policy, context or model creates a new recorded version. A case used to tune that version becomes development evidence.

After the planned batch and any justified correction round, choose one outcome:

1. **Use the existing evidence report.** It provides the useful outcome with less total work.

2. **Retain the bounded advisory assessment.** Its concrete benefit outweighs observed harm and recurring preparation within the tested scope.

3. **Stop at a specific evidence gap.** Name the missing input/adapter and the decision it prevents. Do not automatically create another infrastructure project.

An unsupported positive is a material counterexample. Preserve it and do not keep rerunning until the desired answer appears. If it survives the bounded correction opportunity, the positive-completion path remains unqualified. The research can still close with a narrower supported use or a stop recommendation. Exhausting an allowance never makes a requirement satisfied.

### Structured output: distinguish shape, truth and action

The important weakness is not malformed JSON. D3 already returns well-formed structured results. The [frozen experimental schema and decoder](https://github.com/agent-teams-ai/task-verification/blob/9367ce4e7dee40d076f654397fd535a09e2d6c3a/synthetic/next-family/lib/task.mjs) provide one status, references, explanation and a single correction string for each criterion. That string conflates a required code change, a request for evidence, clarification and optional advice. A composite criterion can contain both a demonstrated violation and an unresolved obligation. Do not convert that into an apparently complete correction request.

Proposed product direction (not an adopted API): preserve authored criterion identities/text. When a criterion has several mandatory obligations, retain independently scoped claims, their evidence references and their remaining unknowns. If clause identities are required, derive them from a reviewed/frozen interpretation of the authored requirement, not an unconstrained model-invented checklist. For a conjunctive requirement, a small deterministic reducer over admitted, applicable judgments can return violated for a demonstrated required contradiction; satisfied only if all required obligations are supported; otherwise inconclusive. Missing/invalid execution and stale evidence remain separate from a semantic inconclusive finding. Alternative/disjunctive or ambiguous requirements need their explicit semantics, not this conjunctive rule. A typed claim is not an admitted fact merely because it is valid JSON or has a real source reference.

For later consumers, keep the following output categories distinct:

| Category | Reader/agent action |
| --- | --- |
| Supported mandatory violation | Correct the named behavior, with a concrete counterexample or sufficient source basis. |
| Missing evidence | Obtain the named applicable observation or source; not automatically rewrite code. |
| Ambiguous authored obligation | Request an owner decision; do not silently weaken or expand it. |
| Optional code-review observation | Retain as advisory, not a completion gate. |

Keep raw claims and contradictory evidence; resolve references against the frozen inventory and candidate, and do not suppress an ordinary OCR finding because of a criteria status. Deduplicating exact identities is deterministic; grouping different statements as one underlying defect requires justified equivalence, not a text-similarity threshold alone. No model may grant execution authority or commit Work. Reuse the [M2 result contract](https://github.com/agent-teams-ai/task-verification/blob/9367ce4e7dee40d076f654397fd535a09e2d6c3a/src/result-contract.ts) and applicability/admission boundaries. For D3 closeout render this distinction in the report; **do not change the frozen wire schema for the remaining recheck**, and do not represent human-adjudicated output as autonomous model quality.
